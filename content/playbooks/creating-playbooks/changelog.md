## 2026-07-09

Initial version. Eight sections across the sense, extract, shape, publish, and govern lifecycle, with two artefacts: the Is It a Playbook test and the Play Template.

Status: provisional throughout. The first three external uses are the test suite.

## 2026-07-09 (later)

Added third artefact: Worked Examples, running the Is It a Playbook test against five well-known collections (Atlassian, USDS, DDaT, GitLab, GOV.UK Service Manual). Added the shape-versus-govern hypothesis arising from the survey.

## 2026-07-09 (later still)

Added Format and Access section: default-to-public play, reading path separated from change path, proposing without tooling, the wizard's tax antipattern, and projections (decks, PDFs, cards) as amplification artefacts that never become canonical.

## 2026-07-09 (evening)

Added the recruitment force to default-to-public and print to the projections list.

## 2026-07-09 (night)

Added two amplification artefacts: an intro slide deck (creating-playbooks-intro.pptx, ten slides, explicitly marked as a projection carrying no status or last-tested date) and a 90-minute back-of-the-room training session guide built on the 4Cs, in which participants run the Is It a Playbook test on the organisation's own documents and write a real play with the template.

## 2026-07-09 (later that night)

Converted the training session to remote delivery over Teams and Mural: six-zone board spec, deck slides embedded as board images instead of screen share, instructions living on the board rather than in the facilitator's voice, breakout mechanics, a producer role above 12 participants, a midpoint break, and a board-stays-open-for-a-week close. Activity time trimmed by five minutes to pay for breakout transitions.

## 2026-07-16

Renamed all files with gapped numeric prefixes (10, 20, 30...) so they sort in reading order in a file explorer, with gaps left for insertions so a new section never triggers a renumber cascade. Added front matter with title, order, and a clean permalink to every page, so the numbers order the vault but never reach a URL. All internal links updated.

## 2026-07-16 (later)

Incorporated GDS's "How we write guidance for the Service Manual" (2018): added the write-for-two-readers candidate play to Shape, added the demand-side pressure (readers and auditors demanding certainty) as a named force in the context-free recipe antipattern, and corrected the Service Manual worked example to carry the tension between GDS's stated pull intent and the assessment gate, rather than the one-sided gate reading.

## 2026-07-16 (slop audit)

Voice audit across the collection. Removed one instance of "leverage", replaced two stock-idiom closers, and thinned intensifiers. Flagged for editorial judgement rather than fixed: the ", not X" appositive appears 38 times and needs thinning by roughly a third, and four aphoristic closers remain, which is the ceiling.

## 2026-07-16 (rename)

Renamed the Shape stage to Codify throughout: filename, permalink, index, stage references, worked examples, and the deck. Codify is the word this collection was defined with from the start, shape was field jargon that crept in. Earlier changelog entries retain the old name, because a changelog that rewrites its own history is not a changelog.

## 2026-07-16 (vocabulary)

Replaced the tactic-noun "move" (as in a chess move) with "action" as the term for a play's second part, everywhere: definition, template, test, stage pages, training session, and the deck. The verb remains in use where something actually moves. Removed the last remaining use of "shape". Earlier changelog entries keep the old vocabulary.

## 2026-07-16 (vocabulary, again)

Removed "load-bearing" from the play anatomy, the retirement test, and the deck. Plainer phrasings in each place.
