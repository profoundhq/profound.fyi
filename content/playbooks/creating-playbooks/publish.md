Publishing a play is when its testing starts. And readers look for plays by the situation they are in.

## Play: publish before you are confident

**When:** a play meets the evidence bar but you still feel it is not ready. That feeling is normal and mostly not information.

**The action:** publish with the provisional status visible. Treat the first three external uses as the test suite. Ask the users what the play failed to tell them, and fold the answers back in.

**Signals:** the play changes within a month of publication. A play that survives its first month untouched was probably not tested. It was just politely ignored.

**When not to:** when the play carries real risk if misapplied and it does not yet say clearly enough when it applies to prevent that.

## Play: index by situation

**When:** the collection is large enough that readers cannot hold it in their heads. Roughly ten plays.

**The action:** organise the collection around the reader's situation. A reader under pressure is searching for "my team keeps committing to work it cannot finish", not for the chapter your play lives in. If each play says clearly when it applies, those situations are the index. Surface them.

**Signals:** readers find plays you did not point them at.

**When not to:** below roughly ten plays, a flat list beats any structure. Do not build the library before you have the books.

## Antipattern: the broadcast

Publishing as pushing. The central team announces the new way, teams comply visibly and ignore it privately.

Good practice spreads by pull. Toyota's word for spreading an improvement is yokoten. It often gets mistranslated as "copy the improvement everywhere", but it means go and see, then adapt: the receiving team pulls the improvement and re-derives it for their context. So the publishing job is to make what worked clear enough that other teams can find it and steal it well, including the conditions it worked under. A team looking to steal an improvement is searching by their situation, which is why this antipattern and the index-by-situation play go together.

If you are pushing plays at people, you have built a compliance system with better typography.
