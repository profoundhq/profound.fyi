---
title: "Reflect back before proposing when a team rejects your first suggestion"
order: 700
updated: 2026-10-12
tags:
  - enabling-teams
---


**When:** You made a suggestion and the team pushed back. You feel the urge to offer a better one straight away.

**The action:** Hold the next idea. Play back what you heard about why the first one didn't fit, and check you have it right.

**Forces:** Offering a quick alternative looks responsive and expert. It also tells the team you weren't listening to the objection. Reflecting back costs a few minutes and some status.

**Signals:** The team corrects or adds to your playback. Your next suggestion is accepted, or the team proposes one themselves.

**When not to:** They rejected it for a clear, stated reason that you understood. Reflecting back then reads as stalling or as a technique being run on them.
