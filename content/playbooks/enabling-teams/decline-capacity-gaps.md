---
title: "Decline when the gap is capacity rather than capability"
order: 300
updated: 2026-10-12
tags:
  - enabling-teams
---


**When:** A team asks for help, but they already know how to do the thing. What they lack is time.

**The action:** Say no. Send the conversation back to whoever sets the team's priorities.

**Forces:** Saying yes feels helpful and builds goodwill. It also turns the enabling team into extra hands, hides a prioritisation problem, and makes the next exit harder. Declining risks the relationship in the short term.

**Signals:** The team's priorities change. The enabling team's queue holds capability requests, and few requests for hands.

**When not to:** A skill gap is hiding behind "no time". Teams often say they lack time when the work is unfamiliar. Test which it is before declining.
