---
title: "Switch to dialogue when the same argument comes back a third time"
order: 600
updated: 2026-10-12
tags:
  - enabling-teams
---


**When:** The team keeps returning to the same disagreement. Each round, people restate their positions more firmly and nobody learns anything new.

**The action:** Name the pattern. Stop trying to win the point, and ask each side to explain the assumptions behind their position until the others can state them back.

**Forces:** Discussion feels productive because it aims at a decision. Dialogue feels slow and can frustrate people who want closure. It risks surfacing tension the team has been avoiding.

**Signals:** People restate each other's assumptions accurately. The argument stops recurring, or comes back in a different form that shows something new was learned.

**When not to:** A decision is genuinely due and someone has the authority to make it. Dialogue then becomes a way of avoiding the call.
