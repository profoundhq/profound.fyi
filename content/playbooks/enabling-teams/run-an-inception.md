---
title: "Run an inception when the team can't state the problem the same way twice"
order: 400
updated: 2026-10-12
tags:
  - enabling-teams
---


**When:** Ask three people on the team what problem they are solving. You get three different answers, and nobody seems bothered by that.

**The action:** Run a short inception to agree the problem, the people it affects, and what the team will not do. Keep it to days.

**Forces:** An inception takes the team away from delivery, and teams under pressure resent that. Skipping it means the disagreement surfaces later, in rework. A long inception slides into big design up front.

**Signals:** Team members describe the problem in matching terms within a week. Fewer backlog items get rejected for being out of scope.

**When not to:** The work continues something the team already understands. An inception there is ceremony. Also skip it if the problem is genuinely unknowable without building something. Build a probe instead.
