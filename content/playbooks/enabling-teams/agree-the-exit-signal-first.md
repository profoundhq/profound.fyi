---
title: "Agree the exit signal before the first session"
order: 100
updated: 2026-10-12
tags:
  - enabling-teams
---


**When:** A stream-aligned team has asked for help with a capability gap. Nobody, including you, can say what "done" would look like.

**The action:** Before any teaching or pairing, agree one sign the team will be able to see when they no longer need you. Write it down where the team can see it.

**Forces:** Teams asking for help want help now, and a conversation about leaving can feel like reluctance. Agreeing the exit costs a session up front. It pays back by giving both sides permission to end the engagement.

**Signals:** The team refers to the exit signal unprompted. Within a few weeks, the practice appears in their own backlog and commits without you present.

**When not to:** The team is firefighting and needs hands immediately. Help first, then agree the exit signal once things are stable enough to talk about.
