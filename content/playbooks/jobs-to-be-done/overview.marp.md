# Jobs to Be Done

Ten plays for finding the progress customers are trying to make

---

## Every play here is still a candidate

Ten plays, all drawn from the published JTBD literature.

Each one carries an external instance from Christensen, Moesta, Ulwick, and others.

None has a first-hand instance yet, and every play says so.

---

## A switch is a contest between four forces

- **Push** of the current situation
- **Pull** of the new solution
- **Anxiety** about the change
- **Habit** of the present

Most teams only add pull. Several plays work on the other three.

---

## Find the job in what people do

- **Interview the switch:** walk recent switchers back through what actually happened
- **Uncover the emotional and social jobs:** probe what people felt and who was watching
- **Watch the workaround:** treat improvised hacks as the most honest signal of demand

---

## Define the market by the job people are doing

- **State the job without the product:** verb, object, and context, true before the product existed
- **Segment by struggling moment:** group customers by the situation they are in
- **Name the real competition:** list everything people hire instead, including doing nothing
- **Target non-consumption:** design for the barrier that keeps people out

---

## Let the job settle what opinion cannot

- **Score the outcomes before the roadmap:** fund what is important and poorly served
- **Lower the barriers to the switch:** remove anxiety and habit instead of adding more pull
- **Anchor strategy on the stable job:** judge each new technology as a candidate hire for the old job

---

## The outstanding work is first-hand evidence

Each play stays a candidate until it has an instance of its own.

The first play to earn one graduates to provisional.
