Last updated 1 November 2026.

Profound is a personal website run by Benedict Steele, who is the data controller. For privacy questions, contact Benedict on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/benedictsteele/).

## What this site processes

**Server logs.** Netlify hosts this site. Like any web host, it processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and keep the service secure. The lawful basis is legitimate interests, namely running a secure website.

**Analytics, only if you accept.** Google Analytics counts page views and shows which playbooks are read. It sets cookies with names starting `_ga`, which can last up to two years, and processes an online identifier and your approximate location. The lawful basis is your consent. If you reject, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, nothing from Google loads.

**Your choice.** Your browser stores your cookie choice and the time you made it, in local storage. This never leaves your device.

**Tools.** The [communication styles](/tools/communication-styles/) tool saves your answers in your browser's local storage so you can return to them. They are not sent to this site. A results link you choose to share carries your answers in the URL.

This site has no accounts, forms that send data, or advertising.

## Who receives data

- [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) hosts the site.
- [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) provides analytics, if you accept.

The [talk pages](/talks/) can embed slides from [Speaker Deck](https://speakerdeck.com/privacy), which receives your IP address when one of those pages loads.

These providers may process data in the United States, under their own safeguards for international transfers.

## How long data is kept

Google Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months. Netlify keeps server logs for the period in its own policy. Your cookie choice stays in your browser until you clear it.

## Your rights

You can change your cookie choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer.

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, and move your personal data, and to object to its use. To use these rights, contact Benedict. You can also complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) in the UK, or to your local data protection authority.
