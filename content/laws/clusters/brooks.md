### Why these together

Four entries from *The Mythical Man-Month* (and *No Silver Bullet*), each a distinct claim. Brooks's Law covers adding people. No Silver Bullet covers essential and accidental complexity. Conceptual Integrity covers design coherence. The Surgical Team covers small expert teams. Together they make Brooks the most-cited author in the index, and half a century after publication the reasons still hold.
