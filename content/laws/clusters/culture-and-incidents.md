*How organisations learn, or fail to, from what goes wrong.*

### Why these together

Westrum classifies the culture. Edmondson names the precondition. Blameless Retros and Just Culture are the practices. Dekker's New View is the deeper claim. The Iceberg explains what leaders do not see. Five Whys is the prompt for the conversation. Five Dysfunctions names the predictable patterns of team failure. Cognitive Load is what culture work protects in the end.
