**How to use it.** In retros, ask "what made this look like the right thing to do at the time?" in place of "who messed up?". The honest answer is almost always missing information, weak feedback loops, or unclear ownership.

**How it gets misused.** Treated as "no consequences ever". Blamelessness applies to the analysis. You can still act on patterns of poor performance and hold blameless retros. They are different conversations in different forums.
