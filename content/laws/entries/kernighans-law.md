Debugging is twice as hard as writing the code. So if you write it as cleverly as you can, you cannot debug it.

**How to use it.** Write at half your maximum cleverness. Leave room for the future debugger, who is you, tired, at 11pm.

**How it gets misused.** Used to refuse all sophistication. The rule is about *needless* cleverness.
