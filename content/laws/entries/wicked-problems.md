**How to use it.** When you cannot get agreement on what the problem is, it is wicked. Stop trying to define it precisely. Instead, run small experiments that reveal different aspects of the problem. Defining the problem is part of the work.

**How it gets misused.** Confused with "hard problems". Hard problems are complicated. Wicked problems are contested. The distinction matters because the methods differ. Complicated problems yield to expertise. Wicked problems require ongoing negotiation.
