Teams perform when they are real teams, with compelling direction, enabling structures, supportive context, and expert coaching.

**How to use it.** Use the five as a diagnostic before launching a team. Most teams fail on the first two conditions. Either they are not really teams (membership unclear, dependencies tangled), or the direction is muddy.

**How it gets misused.** Treated as a checklist. Hackman's work is about the *combination* of conditions and the time they take to develop. Teams take months to develop. Ticking the boxes early and assuming the team is ready is a common failure mode.
