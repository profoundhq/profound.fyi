Three kinds of organisational culture, distinguished by how information flows. Pathological hoards it. Bureaucratic routes it. Generative shares it.

**How to use it.** Watch what happens when bad news arrives. In pathological cultures, messengers are shot. In bureaucratic ones, the news is routed and lost. In generative ones, the news is shared and acted on. The same incident reveals all three.

**How it gets misused.** Used as a label for "good" and "bad" organisations. The typology describes information flow. It does not rank virtue. Bureaucratic cultures can be appropriate, for example in regulated industries. The question is whether the culture fits the work.
