**How to use it.** If you are a leader, assume what you hear is the visible tip. Build channels that surface the rest, such as skip-level meetings, blameless retros, and anonymous feedback. The information exists. The question is whether the system carries it.

**How it gets misused.** Used to demand frontline workers "speak up more". The iceberg comes from the system rather than from frontline reticence. Workers know what happens to messengers in their organisation. The fix is upstream.
