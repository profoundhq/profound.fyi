**How to use it.** Plan for the next reteam before the current team is fully formed. Prefer small, frequent changes (one person rotates) to large, infrequent ones (a reorganisation). A team that survives a deliberate reteam is more resilient than one held together only by stability.

**How it gets misused.** Used to justify constant churn. Reteaming has costs: onboarding, knowledge transfer, and lost context. The discipline is to make changes deliberately and at the right time.
