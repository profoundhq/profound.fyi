**How to use it.** When a problem looks simple, you have probably ignored at least one of the four. Variation explains most operational pain. The "how do we know" lens explains most learning failures.

**How it gets misused.** Cherry-picking a single method, such as Plan-Do-Study-Act, without the rest. The four lenses depend on each other. Variation without the people lens becomes process tyranny. The people lens without variation becomes vibes.
