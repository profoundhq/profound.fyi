**How to use it.** Measure all four together. Shipping often without tracking failures rewards recklessness. Tracking lead time without recovery time hides how fragile you are.

**How it gets misused.** Set as targets. They are diagnostic, like a thermometer. Targeting "ship more often" is the textbook way to break the measurement.
