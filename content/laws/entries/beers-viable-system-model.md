A model for designing organisations that can survive and adapt. Five interdependent systems handle operations, coordination, control, intelligence, and identity.

**How to use it.** Use it to diagnose where an organisation is failing. The problem usually sits in one system: strong operations with no intelligence, or a strong identity with weak coordination. Beer's claim is that all five must be present and connected for the organisation to stay viable under change.

**How it gets misused.** Read as a structural diagram. Beer's model is cybernetic. It describes how information flows between the systems. Drawing the five systems on an org chart and declaring victory is the most common failure.
