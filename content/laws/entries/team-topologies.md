Four kinds of team (working on a stream of value, building a platform, helping others, or owning something complicated). Three ways teams can interact (working together, providing a service, or helping someone learn).

**How to use it.** Default every team to the value-stream type. Justify any other type. Be explicit about how teams interact and for how long.

**How it gets misused.** Renaming existing teams without changing how they work. The topology lives in the interactions, and the labels alone change nothing.
