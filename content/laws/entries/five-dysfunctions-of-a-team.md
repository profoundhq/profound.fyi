**How to use it.** Use it as a diagnostic ladder. The lower dysfunctions enable the higher ones. A team avoiding accountability is usually a team that never really committed, which is usually a team that could not have honest conflict, which is usually a team without trust. Treat the lowest unmet condition.

**How it gets misused.** Treated as evidence-based. Lencioni's model is intuitive but built on consulting experience rather than systematic research. Use it to prompt honest team conversation. It is not a validated framework.
