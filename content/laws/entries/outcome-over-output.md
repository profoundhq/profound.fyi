Build to change something in the world, not to ship a feature.

**How to use it.** Every piece of work should have a specific change in user behaviour, business result, or system property attached. If shipping it would not change any of those, the work is decoration.

**How it gets misused.** Stretched to refuse all delivery commitments. Outputs still matter, because outcomes do not happen without them. The success metric is "something changed because we shipped".
