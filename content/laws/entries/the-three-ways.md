**How to use it.** Use it to diagnose what is missing. Most engineering organisations are good at one of the three and weak at the others. The one you neglect is usually the one limiting your performance.

**How it gets misused.** Treated as a sequence: first flow, then feedback, then learning. The three reinforce each other. Working on one without the others gives short-term gains that decay.
