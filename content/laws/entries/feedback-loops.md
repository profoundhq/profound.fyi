Every system improves at the rate of its slowest feedback loop. Shorten the loops between action and outcome.

**How to use it.** When something is going wrong, look for the missing or delayed feedback. Tests that run overnight delay learning by a day. Customer signals that take a quarter to surface produce a quarter of waste. Most of the cards in this index give feedback-loop advice in different forms.

**How it gets misused.** Treated as "more feedback is better feedback". A loop that fires constantly with noise is worse than no loop at all. The fix is a clear signal, the right frequency, and the right loop for the question. Speed alone is not enough.
