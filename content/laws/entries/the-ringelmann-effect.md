front: "The bigger the group working on a shared task, the less effort each person puts in."
---

**How to use it.** Split shared work into individually owned pieces. Pair people or use small teams. The effect is strongest when contributions are invisible, so make them visible.

**How it gets misused.** Confused with Brooks's Law. Brooks is about coordination cost. Dunbar is about relationship capacity. Ringelmann is about social loafing. They are three different reasons big groups underperform.
