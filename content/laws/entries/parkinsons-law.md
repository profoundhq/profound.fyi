Work expands to fill the time you give it.

**How to use it.** Tighten the definition of "done" before you tighten the deadline. Parkinson bites when "finished" is fuzzy.

**How it gets misused.** Used to justify pretend urgency. Crunch culture is Parkinson's Law applied with malice.
