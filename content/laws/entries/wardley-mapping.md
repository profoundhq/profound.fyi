Plot the things your system depends on. Two axes: how visible they are to the user, and how mature they are. Watch how they move over time.

**How to use it.** Map before you decide on strategy. Most strategy decisions get made without anyone really seeing the situation. Wardley is the discipline of seeing it.

**How it gets misused.** Treated as decoration. The map is the work. A pretty map without a decision is theatre.
