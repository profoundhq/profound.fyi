---

**How to use it.** When someone claims a new tool or method will transform productivity, ask which part of the work it removes: the essential complexity (understanding the problem) or the accidental complexity (typing the code, fighting the tools). Real gains come from the second. The first is irreducible.

**How it gets misused.** Used to refuse all new tools. Brooks argues that the gains are smaller than the marketing claims. He never says they are zero. Compilers, garbage collection, version control, and now better LLMs all genuinely help. They just do not produce miracles.
