front: "We overestimate how much a new technology will change things in the short term. We underestimate how much it will change things in the long term."
---

**How to use it.** For any adoption decision, run two scenarios: what changes in two years, and what changes in five. The answers are often different.

**How it gets misused.** Used to justify any bet on the future. Amara does not say which technologies will pay off, only that some will, eventually.
