---

**How to use it.** When the same problem keeps coming back despite fixes, the structure is producing the behaviour. Map the stocks (what accumulates), the flows (what moves between them), and the feedback loops (what reinforces or balances). The leverage point is usually in the loop structure.

**How it gets misused.** Reduced to causal-loop diagrams without the dynamics. The diagrams are a starting point. The actual modelling needs equations and simulation. Forrester's work is harder than its popularisations suggest.
