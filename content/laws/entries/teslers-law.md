title: Tesler's Law (Conservation of Complexity)
tier: Principle
slug: teslers-law
related: [law-of-leaky-abstractions, no-silver-bullet, kiss]
front: "Every system has some complexity that cannot be removed. You can move it around, but it has to live somewhere."
---

**How to use it.** When someone proposes "simplifying" something, ask who is going to deal with the complexity afterwards. The user? The person running the system? The next engineer? Be honest about the trade.

**How it gets misused.** Used to defend systems that are needlessly complicated. The irreducible part cannot be removed. The accidental mess on top of it can be.
