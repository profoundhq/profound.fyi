Five kinds of situation: clear, complicated, complex, chaotic, and confused. Each one needs a different response.

**How to use it.** Decide which kind of situation you are in *before* picking a method. Use best practice in clear situations, good practice in complicated ones, trial and learning in complex ones, and quick action in chaotic ones.

**How it gets misused.** Treating complex situations with methods built for complicated ones. Most "best practice" failures are mistakes about which situation you are in.
