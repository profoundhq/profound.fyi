Some decisions are easy to undo. Some are not. Spend more time on the ones that are not.

**How to use it.** Ask "if this is wrong, how hard is it to back out". Two-way doors deserve speed: try it, learn, and change your mind. One-way doors deserve care, so get it right before you walk through.

**How it gets misused.** Used to justify rushing everything as "reversible". Most architectural choices, hiring decisions, and public commitments are one-way doors disguised as two-way ones.
