title: Law of Leaky Abstractions

front: "Anything that hides complexity will eventually let some of that complexity show through."
---

**How to use it.** Train people on the layer under the one they work in. Plan time for understanding what is leaking and why.

**How it gets misused.** Used to argue against hiding complexity at all. A leaky abstraction still beats no abstraction. The alternative is everyone working at the lowest level.
