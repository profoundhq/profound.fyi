A simple design passes its tests, makes intent clear, has no duplicated knowledge, and uses the fewest elements possible. The rules apply in that order.

**How to use it.** When two designs both work, walk down the list. The order matters. A small but unclear design is less simple than a larger, obvious one.

**How it gets misused.** Treated as a checklist. The order is the rule. Removing elements before clarifying intent makes things worse.
