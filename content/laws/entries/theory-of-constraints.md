**How to use it.** Find the slowest part. Get the most out of it. Make everything else serve it. Then add more capacity to it. Then start again, because there will be a new slowest part.

**How it gets misused.** Confused with just spotting bottlenecks. The discipline is the order. Get the most out of the constraint *before* spending money to make it bigger. Otherwise the new capacity goes unused.
