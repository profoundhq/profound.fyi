**How to use it.** For any product decision, ask "what job is this being hired to do?". The job is usually broader than the category. Netflix competes with sleep as well as with cinema. The framing changes who the competitors are and which features actually matter.

**How it gets misused.** Reduced to a template. "When I [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]" is a useful sentence. The framework is the discipline of seeing your product through the customer's job.
