If your problem grows when you add more processors, you can get more speed than Amdahl suggests.

**How to use it.** Ask whether the work is fixed or whether it grows with capacity. Fixed work hits Amdahl's limit. Growing work hits Gustafson's, which is more generous.

**How it gets misused.** Used to wave Amdahl away. Both apply, to different kinds of work.
