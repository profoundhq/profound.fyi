A working set of ideas for software practitioners at every level, sorted into six tiers by how solid the evidence is.

## Tiers

- [Laws](/laws/tiers/laws/) - proved by maths or strong, repeated evidence
- [Principles](/laws/tiers/principles/) - design rules that work in most situations
- [Patterns](/laws/tiers/patterns/) - regularities we keep seeing, useful for spotting problems
- [Practices](/laws/tiers/practices/) - ways of working, repeated until habit
- [Sayings](/laws/tiers/sayings/) - sharp phrases that make you think
- [Frameworks](/laws/tiers/frameworks/) - ways of seeing the whole picture

## Clusters

The tiers sort entries by how strong the evidence is. The clusters cut across the tiers and group entries that work on the same concern.

- [The Adoption cluster](/laws/clusters/adoption/) - *Why technology arrives unevenly, and what to do about it.*
- [The Measurement cluster](/laws/clusters/measurement/) - *How to measure without breaking the things you measure.*
- [The Improvement cluster](/laws/clusters/improvement/) - *How systems get better over time.*
- [The Distributed Systems cluster](/laws/clusters/distributed-systems/) - *The mathematics of what is possible across a network.*
- [The Interface Contracts cluster](/laws/clusters/interface-contracts/) - *What you promise, what you accept, what your users come to depend on.*
- [The Team Design cluster](/laws/clusters/team-design/) - *How to organise people for software work.*
- [The Culture and Incidents cluster](/laws/clusters/culture-and-incidents/) - *How organisations learn, or fail to, from what goes wrong.*
- [The Systems Thinking cluster](/laws/clusters/systems-thinking/) - *Ways of seeing systems whole.*
- [The Lean Product cluster](/laws/clusters/lean-product/) - *How to build the right thing.*
- [The Code Design cluster](/laws/clusters/code-design/) - *How to write code that survives change.*
- [The Brooks cluster](/laws/clusters/brooks/) - *One author whose work shapes most of the field.*
