Design rules with strong evidence and well-understood failure modes. Reliable in most situations.

**How to use them.** Use them as the default position. If you choose to depart from a principle, write down why. The discipline lies in the justification.

**How they get misused.** Applied without judgement. SOLID applied to every class produces unreadable code. The Testing Pyramid applied without thought produces brittle test suites. A principle followed mechanically becomes a ritual.
