Proved by maths or strong, repeated evidence. Violating them produces predictable, measurable failure.

**How to use them.** Treat them as constraints. When a plan ignores a law, the plan is wrong. Use them to set the limits of what is possible before you start designing within those limits.

**How they get misused.** Stretched beyond their domain. Amdahl's Law applies to parallel computation, and says nothing about teams. CAP applies to distributed data, and only to that. Each law has a scope, and outside that scope it stops being a law.
