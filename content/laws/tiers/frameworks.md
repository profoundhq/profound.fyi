Ways of seeing the whole picture. They give you better questions to ask.

**How to use them.** Use one when you are stuck because the problem is the wrong shape. Cynefin asks "what kind of situation is this?" Wardley asks "where are we, and where is this moving?" Theory of Constraints asks "what is the slowest part?" The framework changes the question, and a better question changes the work.

**How they get misused.** Treated as a summary. Nobody learns a framework from a one-line description. The entry points you at the framework. The work is reading the source, practising the move, and getting it wrong a few times.
