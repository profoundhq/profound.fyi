After an incident or failure, the people involved meet to reconstruct what happened, why their actions made sense at the time, and what to change. The aim is to understand the system that allowed the failure rather than to find someone to blame. People only speak openly when they trust the blameless promise, so leaders need to keep it, especially the first time something goes badly wrong. Share the write-up beyond the team so others can learn from it too.
