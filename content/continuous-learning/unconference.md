A participant-led event where attendees set the agenda on the day. People propose sessions, everyone chooses where to go, and anyone can leave a session that is not useful to them. Open Space Technology is a well-known format. Unconferences bring out the topics people care about and spread learning across an organisation. They need good facilitation and a clear overall theme.

- [What is Open Space Technology?](https://openspaceworld.org/wp2/what-is/) (Open Space World)
