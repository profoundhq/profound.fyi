A team shows working software or other outputs to stakeholders and colleagues, usually at the end of an iteration. Demos create a regular point for feedback and let people outside the team see what is being built and why. Show real, working things in preference to slides, and invite questions. Rotating who presents helps everyone in the team understand the whole.
