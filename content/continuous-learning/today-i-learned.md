People share small things they have learned, in a dedicated channel, a shared document, or a regular slot in a meeting. Sharing small lessons normalises learning in public and lets knowledge spread casually. Keep the bar low so people share often, and welcome posts about mistakes and surprises as much as clever discoveries.
