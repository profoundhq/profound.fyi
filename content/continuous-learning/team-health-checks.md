A team regularly rates itself against a set of factors, such as pace, quality, fun, learning, and support, and discusses the results. Health checks start conversations about problems that might otherwise go unspoken, and show trends over time. Use them for discussion within the team. Comparing teams on their scores quickly makes the scores dishonest.
