A coach works with one person over a series of conversations to help them think through goals, problems, and their own ways of working. The coach mostly asks rather than tells, so the learning belongs to the person being coached. It suits development that is personal or sensitive, and works best when the person chooses the topic. Keep coaching separate from line management where you can, because people are less candid with someone who also assesses them.
