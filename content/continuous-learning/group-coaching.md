A coach works with a small group of people, often with similar roles or challenges, who are not necessarily in the same team. Members learn from the coach and from each other's situations. Group coaching reaches more people than one-to-one coaching and builds peer support. Agree confidentiality at the start, and keep the group small enough that everyone has time to speak.
