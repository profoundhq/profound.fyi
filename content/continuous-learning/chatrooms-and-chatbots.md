Chat tools let people ask questions and share what they know in the flow of work. Topic channels make answers searchable, and chatbots can point people to documentation or answer frequent questions. Chat is fast but forgetful, so copy answers that keep coming up into a wiki or other lasting home. Check chatbot answers regularly, because a confident wrong answer teaches the wrong thing.
