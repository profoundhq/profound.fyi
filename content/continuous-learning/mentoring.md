A more experienced person supports someone's development through regular conversations, sharing advice, experience, and connections. Mentors offer their own knowledge more directly than coaches do. Mentoring helps people learn the unwritten parts of a role or organisation. Let people choose their mentors where possible, and agree how often you will meet and what you will focus on. Mentors learn too, and reverse mentoring, where junior people mentor senior people, can be just as valuable.
