Telling someone how their work or behaviour affected you, and hearing the same about yourself. Regular, specific feedback is one of the quickest ways to learn, but it depends on trust and skill on both sides. Describe what you observed and its effect, and avoid judging the person. Practise receiving feedback with curiosity, and make it a habit instead of something saved up for reviews.
