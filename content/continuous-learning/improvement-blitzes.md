A short, focused burst of effort, typically a few days, in which a cross-functional group improves a specific process or problem. They are also known as kaizen events or rapid improvement events. The group learns by observing the current process, testing changes, and measuring the results. Plan the follow-up before the blitz starts, as improvements often slip back without someone owning them.

- [Kaizen](https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/kaizen/) (Lean Enterprise Institute)
