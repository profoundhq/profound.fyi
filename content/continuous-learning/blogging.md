Writing, internally or in public, about what you are working on and learning. Explaining something to readers forces you to understand it, and a post outlasts the conversation it came from. Team blogs and weeknotes make work visible across an organisation. Lower the bar to publishing, since short posts count, and agree what can be shared outside before anyone writes about client or sensitive work.
