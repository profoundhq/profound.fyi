People who share a craft or interest across team boundaries meet regularly to learn from each other, for example testers, designers, or people working with a particular technology. They spread good practice and give specialists peers outside their own team. They work best when membership is voluntary and members set the agenda. Give them time and a little support, since communities that run entirely on goodwill tend to fade.

- [Introduction to communities of practice](https://www.wenger-trayner.com/introduction-to-communities-of-practice/) (Etienne and Beverly Wenger-Trayner)
