A dojo is a dedicated space and time to practise skills on realistic problems, away from delivery pressure. Coding dojos often use katas, small exercises repeated to build fluency. Some organisations run immersive dojos where a team brings its own backlog and works with coaches for several weeks. Keep the focus on practice over output, and make it safe to try things and fail.

- [Coding Dojo](https://codingdojo.org/) (codingdojo.org)
