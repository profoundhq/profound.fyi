The whole team works on the same thing, at the same time, at the same computer. One person types while the others think, discuss, and direct, and the roles rotate every few minutes. Mobbing spreads knowledge across the team quickly and avoids hand-offs. It can be tiring, so take regular breaks, and it does not suit every task. It is also known as ensemble or mob programming.
