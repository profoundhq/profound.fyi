A team regularly pauses to reflect on how it has been working and agree what to change. Retrospectives are the core learning loop of agile teams. Vary the format to keep them fresh, and make sure everyone can speak. Limit actions to a small number the team will actually complete, and check progress on them at the next retrospective, or people lose faith in the process.

- [Retromat](https://retromat.org/) (activities for retrospectives)
- [FunRetrospectives](https://www.funretrospectives.com/) (retrospective formats and activities)
