Spending time observing someone in a different role or team as they do their work. Shadowing builds empathy and understanding of how other parts of the organisation work, and can help people explore a change of career. Agree what the shadow will see and when they can ask questions. Reciprocal shadowing, where both people take a turn, doubles the learning.
