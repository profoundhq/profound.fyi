Reading books, articles, papers, and documentation to learn independently. Reading is flexible and cheap, and brings in ideas from outside the organisation. Sharing what you read through book clubs, reading lists, or short summaries in a team channel multiplies its value. Make reading during working hours a legitimate use of time.
